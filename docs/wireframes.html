Deskaway wireframes

Airbnb-style layouts for the five screens, desktop (1280 px) and mobile (375 px): photo-first cards without borders, a search pill, filter chips and a floating booking card. Grey boxes are placeholders; blue notes describe behaviour. Colours, spacing and type come from the design tokens.

1. Search: /

Search pill for where, when and who; chips for the work filters. Cards show the price per night only, no total. Everything lives in the URL.
